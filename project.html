Frequently Asked Questions

How soon does the landlord receive rent?

Instant transfers via UPI and IMPS hit the landlord's verified account in under 15 minutes. Credit card payments take 1-2 hours depending on bank verification.

Is the HRA Receipt valid for income tax?

Yes, our receipts contain landlord PAN, rental property address, and computer-verified UTR transaction numbers compliant with Section 10(13A).

Are there additional convenience fees?

UPI payments have a 0% convenience fee. Credit card transfers have a standard 1% fee which is often covered by reward points and card bonuses.
